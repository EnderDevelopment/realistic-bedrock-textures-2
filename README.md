# RealisticBedrock

RealisticBedrock is a Minecraft Bedrock Edition texture pack designed to enhance the visual appeal of your game with realistic textures. This pack includes detailed and realistic textures for various blocks, such as dirt, grass, leaves, logs, and stone, making your Minecraft world look more immersive and lifelike. Perfect for players who want a more realistic visual experience in Minecraft Bedrock Edition 1.26.40 and above.

## Install

1. Download the RealisticBedrock texture pack from the GitHub repository or the EnderDevelopment marketplace.
2. Open Minecraft Bedrock Edition.
3. Go to the "Settings" menu.
4. Select "Resource Packs" from the left-hand menu.
5. Click on the "Import" button at the bottom of the screen.
6. Navigate to the location where you downloaded the RealisticBedrock texture pack and select it.
7. Click "Open" to import the texture pack.
8. Enable the RealisticBedrock texture pack by toggling the switch next to it.

## Use

1. Start a new world or load an existing one in Minecraft Bedrock Edition.
2. The RealisticBedrock textures will automatically apply to the blocks in your world.
3. Explore your Minecraft world and enjoy the enhanced visual experience with realistic textures.
